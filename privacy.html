Privacy Policy

Take the Floor · Last updated November 12, 2026

Take the Floor is a research tool from the Technion for practicing English presentations in virtual reality. It has two parts: the VR app that students use, and a teacher dashboard. This policy explains what each part collects, why, and who can see it.

Information we collect

In the VR app

  • Your voice. While you practice, the app listens through the microphone so the audience can react and you get feedback. Speech is turned into text on the computer running the session. Audio recordings are kept only if recording is switched on for that session.
  • Practice measurements. Time spent on each slide and where your head was facing (toward the audience, down or away). This is head direction, not eye tracking.
  • Your name and email, when they appear on the first slide of your presentation, so the report can be filed and sent to you.
  • Your Meta account, if you are signed in on the headset, is used only to show your Meta avatar. We receive your Meta user ID and avatar, not your contacts or activity.

In the teacher dashboard

  • The Google account email of teachers who sign in, to check that they were invited.
  • Presentations, practice reports, recordings, courses and settings that teachers upload or save.

How we use it

  • To run the practice session, including audience questions and reactions.
  • To produce a practice report for the student and their teacher.
  • To let invited teachers manage courses, presentations and practice settings.
  • For research on presentation practice, only in de-identified form unless you agreed otherwise.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Services that process data for us

  • Google Gemini receives the text of what you said (never the audio) to generate audience questions and written feedback.
  • Google Drive and Gmail (account vrdevicetechnion@gmail.com) store dashboard files and send reports and invitations.
  • Google Sign-In confirms a teacher's email address. The dashboard asks Google only for your email address and access to the files the dashboard itself creates.
  • Render hosts the teacher dashboard.
  • Meta provides avatars on Quest headsets.

Each service handles data under its own privacy terms.

Who can see your data

Only teachers invited to the dashboard and the research team. Teachers sign in with Google, and access can be removed at any time. Reports are emailed only when a teacher or student chooses to send them.

How long we keep it

Practice reports are kept for the duration of the course and the related research. Teachers can archive or permanently delete recordings and presentations from the dashboard. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices

  • You can practice with recording switched off.
  • You can leave your name and email off the first slide.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us.

Security

The dashboard is available only to invited Google accounts, uses encrypted connections, and keeps its files in a private Google Drive. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
